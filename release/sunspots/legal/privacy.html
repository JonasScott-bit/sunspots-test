Short version: Sunspots doesn't ask who you are, doesn't track you, and doesn't show ads. Your game save stays on your own device.

Who we are

Sunspots is made by Ethereal Entertainment Systems ("EES", "we"), based in Manitoba, Canada. Questions about privacy: [support email].

What the game keeps, and where

  • Your save and settings. Your progress (Sunspots, Nights, missions), settings (sound, quality, text size) and the page you were on in the manual are stored in your browser's local storage and IndexedDB on your device. They are not sent to us.
  • No accounts. There's no sign-up, login or profile.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. The game has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party trackers, and it sets no tracking cookies.
  • Offline files. If you install the game to your home screen, a service worker keeps copies of the game files on your device so it starts faster and works offline.
